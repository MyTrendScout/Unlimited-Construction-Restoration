What's Different About Building in Myakka City

Permits go through Manatee County. Myakka City is an unincorporated community, not a city with its own building department. Permits, plan review and inspections are handled by Manatee County's building and development services. Rules and fees change, so always confirm current requirements with the county for your exact address. We handle this process for our clients.

Well and septic are part of the plan. Many Myakka City properties are not on city water or sewer. Where a new well or septic system is needed, it has its own permitting and approvals, and its location affects where the house can go. We plan around it from the first site visit.

Low ground and flood zones. The Myakka River and the wetlands around it mean some parcels have low areas or fall in flood zones that can affect elevation, foundation design and drainage. We check the flood designation early and design to meet it. For flood insurance or elevation questions, talk to your insurer and the county.

Wind design still applies. Being inland doesn't exempt a home from Florida's wind-load requirements. Windows, doors, roof connections and structural details have to meet the current Florida Building Code.

Access and distance. Long drives, soft ground in the rainy season and deliveries of concrete and materials all need planning on rural lots. We look at site access before scheduling, not after the trucks are booked.

How a Project With Us Works

  1. Free estimate and site visit. We walk the property, listen to your goals and point out anything about the land that will affect the work.
  2. Design. We work out layout, materials and budget with you. Where structural engineering is needed, we coordinate with a licensed engineer.
  3. Written estimate. You get a clear scope of work in writing, so you know what's included before anything begins.
  4. Permits and approvals. We submit to Manatee County and coordinate the well and septic pieces where they apply.
  5. Construction. Our crew and trusted subcontractors build to the approved plans, and we keep you updated as the job moves forward.
  6. Inspections and walkthrough. We schedule the required county inspections and walk the finished project with you.

We work in English and Spanish (hablamos español) with homeowners, crews, suppliers and inspectors, so everyone stays on the same page.

Common Questions From Myakka City Homeowners

Do I get my permit from a city or the county?

Manatee County, because Myakka City isn't an incorporated city. As your contractor, we normally pull the permits.

Can you build on land without city water or sewer?

Often, yes. Well, septic, access and drainage all have to be planned and approved, so we review them during the site visit and design stage.

How do I check that a contractor is licensed?

Look up the license number on the Florida DBPR license search. Ours is CBC1261852.

How long will a new home on acreage take?

It depends on the design, county review, well and septic approvals, material availability and weather. We'll explain the schedule factors for your project in your written estimate instead of guessing up front.
