About UCR
Bradenton Built. Bradenton Based.
Jesus Noe Salinas Cervantes
Owner · Certified Building Contractor
License CBC1261852

Unlimited Construction & Restoration isn't a franchise with a call center in another state. It's Jesus Salinas and his crew, working out of Bradenton for 21 years.

That matters more than it sounds. We know which inspectors want what. We know how Manatee County handles flood zone permits, because we've spent two decades pulling them. We know what a Gulf Coast summer does to a slab poured wrong. That isn't something you learn from a corporate playbook — it's something you learn from doing the work here, year after year.

We work in English and Spanish, on both sides of every job — homeowners, subs, suppliers, inspectors. Nobody on our projects gets left out of the conversation because of language, and nothing gets lost between the plan and the pour.
